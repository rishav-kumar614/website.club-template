Our Story

Born from the Heart of Naples

Our journey began in 1987 when Chef Marco Ferretti brought the ancient art of Neapolitan pizza-making to life. Using time-honored recipes passed down through generations, we hand-stretch every dough, source the finest San Marzano tomatoes, and fire each pizza in our beloved wood-burning oven.

Today, Inferno Pizza stands as a testament to that original passion — a place where every bite transports you to the sun-drenched piazzas of southern Italy.
